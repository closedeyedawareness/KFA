About me

“Where silence becomes sound, and sound becomes the key”.

My name is Adelmar van Dijk, a professional pianist, composer and producer from The Netherlands. Keys From Above is my creative expression and the space where my inner world meets sound. I believe music can be more than something we listen to — it can be a key that opens doors within us. I don't write music from theory or structure. I write from what I feel, in the moment it arrives.

Every piece begins as a state — something lived, something seen, something that moves through me. I don't try to control it or shape it into expectations. I follow it, translate it, and let it become sound. That's why no two tracks are the same.

Some are light and weightless, like movement without gravity. Others carry tension, break open, or search for something beneath the surface. And some simply arrive — calm, grounded, complete. Together, they are not different styles. They are different expressions of the same source.

My music isn't built to fit into a genre. It moves through emotional states — and invites the listener to recognize something within themselves. I don't create for numbers or systems. I create because something wants to be heard. And if it resonates — it was meant to.

Adelmar

Meditations

“My work lives in the space between silence and sound. I create meditations, soundscapes, and frequency-based compositions designed to support presence, emotional balance, and inner stillness.

I've always been drawn to the way sound affects the body before the mind has time to interpret it. A single tone can slow the breath. A gentle progression can soften tension. A carefully shaped frequency can invite the nervous system to settle. That understanding guides everything I create.

My meditative soundscapes are designed to be entered. They support practices like meditation, breathwork, reflection, rest, and creative focus. They're not about forcing an experience, but about allowing one — giving the listener room to meet themselves where they are.”

Adelmar van Dijk
